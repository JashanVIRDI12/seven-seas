# Seven Sea

## Register

brand

## Users

Commercial drivers, owner-operators and fleet coordinators in Prince George,
British Columbia. A driver may be looking for help beside a stopped truck on a
phone; a fleet coordinator may be planning service from a desktop.

## Product Purpose

Make the repair shop easy to assess, call and find. The supplied client brief
states the central idea: “KEEP THE NORTH MOVING.” The primary action is calling
the verified shop phone. A booking is arranged by phone; the site cannot confirm
appointments automatically.

## Brand Personality

Premium, simple, local, dependable, quick to act. Red and blue. The first build's
"industrial editorial" personality was rejected by the client on 2026-10-02;
see BRIEF.md.

## Anti-references

WebGL or fake 3D trucks, dense editorial layouts, all-caps label chrome,
identical SaaS card grids, glassmorphism, neon gradients, mechanic clip art,
Canadian clichés, invented reviews or statistics, and motion without a job.
Cards are welcome when each one carries a distinct decision or fact.

## Design Principles

1. The call is always one tap away.
2. One motion language: inset frames that open, type that rises from masks.
3. One memorable peak: the truck driving the road back.
4. Every card answers one question.
5. Publish verified facts; keep unknown business data unset.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The supplied brief requires semantic HTML, keyboard navigation, visible focus,
adequate contrast, usable native scrolling and a complete reduced-motion view.
Provide large touch targets, mobile call access and no dependence on hover.

## Evidence

Client: the supplied complete creative brief. Contacts: the Google Maps listing
documented in docs/business-contact-sources.md. The sibling sevenseas prototype
uses Next.js, but its 3D assets and unverified service copy do not fit this brief.
This workspace had no existing site, photos, fonts or client logo.
